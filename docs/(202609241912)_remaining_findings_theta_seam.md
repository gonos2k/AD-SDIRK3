## This candidate: source and numerical check

For a physical-dimension whole tile, the C++ scalar helper previously computed only interior X faces and left `H1[0]` and `H1[nx]` zero even when X was periodic. In the original Fortran `horizontal_diffusion_s`, periodic X retains the full `i_start..i_end+1` face loop and consumes the two seam faces in the first/last cell divergence. The corrected C++ seam uses the same local interpolation order as its interior faces:

`Hseam = <msftx>/<msfty> · <Kh> · <MUT> · rdx · (q_first−q_last)`.

Both physical-dimension endpoint aliases receive this oriented value. For WRF's packed whole-domain layout, the last mass column aliases the first: interior face `H1[nx−1]` is already the unique seam; `H1[0]` copies it and `H1[nx]` copies `H1[1]` for the duplicate cell. The change is limited to a single complete periodic-X tile, since a partial tile cannot infer its neighbor from local first/last cells. The helper's mass-map face approximation and missing terrain metric terms remain F1/G1 work.

The hybrid stage-1 fixture now has nonzero U and V with zero Y-wall normal velocity. Its captured face records have nonzero X/Y advection, vertical advection, and X/Y diffusion signals. Reconstructing the code's native face divergence gives maximum slow coupled-theta error `9.9243e-5`. The fast no-face residual is `5.47555e-5`; it is screened with a gross FP32 scale from the slow fluxes and is **not** an independently attributed fast source budget. The periodic advection seam, diffusion seam alias, Y walls and vertical endpoints satisfy their supported rules. The diffusion seam flux signal is `35147.2` in the helper's native coupled flux units, versus independent wrapped-formula error `0.00256348`; omitting it changes the boundary tendency by `40.9957` in this nonunit-map fixture.
