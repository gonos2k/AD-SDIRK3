# Dry option-2 scalar metric diffusion in the actual Step 9 RHS

## Context and change

This candidate starts from PR #229 head `bd17679c83a256a0c4c0d7a12d61d37f7d8a8608`. That PR source-matched a private C++ helper to Fortran `horizontal_diffusion_s`, but production Step 9 still used the legacy scalar helper. This change connects the new helper only for canonical, single-rank, complete periodic-X/symmetric-Y tiles with WRF-declared `km_opt=1`, `damp_opt=0`, dry state, WRF-provided eta weights, constant namelist Kh, no coefficient override and no theta-face capture or coupled-slow export. For this coefficient policy, Fortran `isotropic_km` gives `xkhh=khdif/prandtl=3*khdif` with `prandtl=1/3`.

At each RHS evaluation Step 9 now derives `z_w=(PH+PHB)/g`, positive `rdzw=1/(z_{k+1}-z_k)`, backward X face slopes with a periodic seam, and backward Y face slopes with zero symmetric-wall faces from the **current stage state**. It restores signed negative `dnw/dn` from the C++ storage of positive reciprocal magnitudes, leaving the invalid bottom `dn(1)` as a zero sentinel, and derives Fortran `cf1–3` from `dnw(1),dn(2:3),fnm/fnp(2)`. On WRF packed arrays it slices the unique physical mass core, calls the exact helper, then restores the periodic X and symmetric Y aliases. The raw helper result is added once to coupled `t_tend`; the existing canonical conversion then divides the diffusion increment by hybrid layer mass L.

The main-thread Fortran zero-copy preflight now rejects `diff_opt=2` with active `khdif` **or** `kvdif` if microphysics is active or owned QV is nonzero. This inspects the actual owned rank patch before any OpenMP tile worker calls C++; the ABI's `C_NULL_PTR,0` moisture arguments alone are not treated as proof of a dry state. The edited authoritative source is `dyn_em/module_implicit_sdirk3.F`. Canonical C++ calls with positive WRF km-opt metadata fail closed when they do not meet the bounded option-2 scalar contract. Metadata-absent test/API contexts and noncanonical configurations retain the pre-existing legacy helper and remain **unvalidated**, not silently included in this parity claim.

## Remaining checklist

This closes a **bounded dry option-2 scalar Step 9 production–consumer connection**, not full L34. The public ABI still does not provide spatially varying `xkhh`, moisture, or general physical/intertile halo fields. Other `km_opt`/damping choices, noncanonical coordinate modes, supplied coefficients, face-budget capture, MPI/partial tiles, option-2 U/V/W stresses, coupled-slow export and the full historical Fortran time step remain unvalidated. Repeated runtime and same-accuracy temporal convergence have not been measured; the helper performs several whole-tensor input checks and CPU coefficient reads per RHS and may add synchronization cost. Active option-2 diffusion's full-step VJP/observation-path adjoint and the legacy WRFPLUS/Tapenade TL/AD mismatch remain open. The next discriminating work is same-stage variable-K/rho/map and halo ownership parity, followed by active-step derivative and time-refinement checks; no global option-2 or DA readiness claim follows from this one-step run.
